FAQ

Good to know

What are instant wicks?

Wicks are thick, mature-looking locs. With instant wicking, your hair is formed into finished wicks in one session, so you leave with the look on day one instead of waiting months for the hair to lock.

How does pricing work?

Every look is custom, so we quote privately by text once we know your style, length and location. Hair is included on wicks and big locs.

Do I need to buy hair?

Tell us the style when you book. We'll confirm the type and amount of hair by text, or bring it for you if you prefer.

Where do you travel?

Anywhere in Los Angeles and nearby cities: homes, hotels, sets and venues. Farther trips are welcome; just ask.

Do you style kids and men?

Yes. Kids get gentle, patient sessions at home, and men's stitch braids and cornrows are a regular part of the book.
